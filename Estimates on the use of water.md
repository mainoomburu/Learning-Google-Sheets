While working on this current file, I had to solve a few questions concerning the levels of water access on different regions.
It was not an easy journey as at times I was stuck trying to figure out the best way to solve a particular question, or
what would be the best chart to use to visualize data. I have come to understand the different factors to look before deciding
on my visualization.
  1) Is it a comparison - am I comparing different categories
  2) Is it a composition - how do the individual components combine to make a whole
  3) Is it a relationship - can one value be used to predict the value of another
  4) Is it a distribution - what is the spread

What am I measuring:
  1) Am I measuring the frequency - number of times a value occurs
  2) Am I measuring the central tendency - where the majority of our data lies
  3) Am I measuring the variability - how spread out the data is

In this project, I focused on composition using a stacked bar chart, comparison using a line chart and distribution using a
box and whisker plot.
