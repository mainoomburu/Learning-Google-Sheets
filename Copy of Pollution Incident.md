In this exercise, I opt to use a Large Language Model(LLM) as a study guide, 
not just using it to give me the answers but to use it only when I am stuck with certain parts of the exercise. 
The biggest help for me was in these 2 key instances:
  1) Writing the formula for mode when there is a filter. I came to understand a new way to solve future issues that require me to reference
     one column and use a second column to apply a filter.
  2) The second bit was, as I was creating a table for calculating my quartiles, one of my quartile columns was under the date
     column and it was challenging to understand why the returned solution was returning a date and not a value. Gemini helped me understand
     the importance of checking the data type in my cell and changing when necessary.
